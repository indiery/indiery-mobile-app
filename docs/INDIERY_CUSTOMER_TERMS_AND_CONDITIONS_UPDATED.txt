A Partner arriving late does not create a waiting charge against the Customer. Waiting starts only after the Partner has actually recorded arrival at pickup.

23. CUSTOMER CANCELLATION

The following cancellation rule applies to the normal Customer app flow:

A. Before pickup

- Cancellation is free.
- No cancellation charge applies.
- An eligible prepaid amount is refunded to the Indiery Wallet.
- Eligible Coins originally applied to the Order are returned.

B. From pickup through exactly 5 minutes after pickup

- Cancellation remains free.
- No cancellation charge applies.
- An eligible prepaid amount is refunded to the Indiery Wallet.
- Eligible Coins originally applied to the Order are returned.
- Any separately accrued waiting charge remains payable and is not converted into a cash refund.

C. More than 5 minutes after pickup and before delivery

- The Customer may cancel through the supported app flow.
- The cancellation charge is 10% of the current Order Total.
- For a prepaid Order, the charge is retained from the captured amount and the eligible balance is credited to the Indiery Wallet.
- If the prepaid amount does not fully cover the charge, the remaining supported amount may be debited from Indiery Coins.
- For COD before cash collection, the applicable charge is debited from Indiery Coins, subject to the minus INR 50 Coin limit.
- Eligible Coins originally applied to the Order are returned, while separately accrued waiting charges remain payable.

D. After delivery

- Cancellation is unavailable.
- The Customer may still report a duplicate payment, incorrect charge, loss, damage, fraud, or delivery dispute through support.

The pickup time used for this calculation is the "Picked up" timestamp recorded by the platform. The Customer sees the applicable charge before confirming cancellation where the app has current Order data.
