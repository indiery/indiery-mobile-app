1. How do I book a vehicle on demand with Indiery?

You can hire Bikes, Mini Trucks (Tata Ace), or Large Trucks on-demand via our app or web platform in under 90 seconds. Simply enter pickup and drop addresses, choose your vehicle size, and our system dispatches the nearest verified driver with upfront pricing.

2. How does Live GPS Tracking work for senders and receivers?

As soon as your driver partner accepts the trip, a real-time GPS tracking link is generated. Both sender and receiver can view the truck moving live on a map with updated ETA and exact speed from pickup to final doorstep.

3. Do you provide full House & Office Shifting (Packers & Movers)?

Yes! Our Packers & Movers service covers complete 1 BHK to 4+ BHK apartment and corporate office relocations. It includes professional packing boxes, furniture dismantling, loading, secure transit, and careful doorstep unloading.

4. Are trip fares transparent with zero hidden charges?

Yes, 100%. Indiery calculates and locks trip fares upfront before you confirm. Fares include fuel, distance, and base vehicle fee. You will never face surprise surges, toll markups, or hidden costs.

5. How can vehicle owners or drivers register and earn daily income?

Vehicle owners and drivers can register their two-wheeler, Tata Ace, pickup, or commercial truck on our Driver Earning Platform. Get verified within 24 hours and start accepting on-demand delivery orders with instant daily bank payouts.
